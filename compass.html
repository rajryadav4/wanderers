Steel buildings and laptops bend a phone's compass. Put the centre crosshair on something you can actually see, then tap it. One tap fixes direction and tilt.

Where the numbers come from

Motion: the gyroscope drives every movement; the compass only sets north, averaged while the phone lies flat and corrected slowly after (sensor fusion, tested on simulated noisy sensors: 0.1–0.5° error, 0.1° wobble). Sky: J2000 positions precessed to today (IAU 1976), UTC time and Earth's rotation (IAU 1982 sidereal time) at your GPS position, refraction (Saemundsson). Magnetic north to true north: NOAA/BGS World Magnetic Model 2025. Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble via NASA GIBS. Cities: GeoNames.
