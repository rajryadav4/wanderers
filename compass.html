Sky Compass

Point your phone's camera at the sky. WANDERERS works out where you are pointing from your location, the time and your phone's compass and tilt, then guides you to an object we have investigated with NASA data.

  • Works indoors too: the walls don't matter, only the direction.
  • Objects below the horizon are shown as well. Point at the floor to find them.
  • Location and motion data stay on your phone.
